## 简体中文

将爆弹改为粗略的同步搜索与放置。普通区块只使用 25 根代表柱，中心区块最多 36 根，大幅加快处理。Boss Bar 改为只显示火把放置进度。

## English

Reworked bombs into a coarse streaming scan-and-place flow. Normal chunks sample 25 representative columns and the origin chunk samples at most 36, greatly improving speed. The Boss Bar now shows torch-placement progress only.
